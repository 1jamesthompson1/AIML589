Subject: Have your say on AI values — 15-min survey for New Zealanders

Kia ora,

We're a research team at Te Herenga Waka — Victoria University of Wellington
studying how AI systems like ChatGPT make decisions. Right now, these systems
reflect values set by overseas tech companies, not New Zealanders.

We want to change that.

We've trained an AI model on New Zealand value data from the World Values
Survey. Now we need the public's help to evaluate it. We're looking for
people aged 18+ living in New Zealand to complete a short anonymous survey.

Regardless on your views of AI (Love, Hate or indifference) we are keen to hear from you. Different perspectives make the research stronger.

What's involved?
- You'll read a few short scenarios where AI makes decisions
- You'll rate how much you agree with the actions of the AI
- You'll answer a few questions about your own values
- Takes about 15 minutes

Your responses are completely anonymous. No personal information is collected.
You can optionally enter a separate prize draw for one of two $50 Prezzy Cards.
To enter, you will provide an email address in a separate form. This email
address will not be linked to your survey responses.

How your responses will be used
- We'll analyse the data to see which AI models people prefer
- All data will be made public in anonymised form so the research
  community can use it
- The goal is to develop a practical method for aligning AI to public values—a way for governments and communities to shape AI behaviour rather than
  leaving it to private companies alone
