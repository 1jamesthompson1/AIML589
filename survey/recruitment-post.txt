Subject: Have your say on AI values — 15-min survey for New Zealanders with prize pool

Kia ora,

We're a research team at Te Herenga Waka — Victoria University of Wellington
studying how AI systems like ChatGPT make decisions. Right now, these systems
reflect values set by overseas tech companies, not New Zealanders.

We've trained some new models and we want to know if the public prefer them. We're looking for people aged 18+ to complete a short anonymous survey to provide your value judgements on AI's actions in simulated scenarios.

Regardless on your views of AI (Love, Hate or indifference) we are keen to hear from you. Different perspectives make the research stronger.

On completion of the survey you may optionally enter a separate prize draw for one of two $50 Prezzy Cards.
