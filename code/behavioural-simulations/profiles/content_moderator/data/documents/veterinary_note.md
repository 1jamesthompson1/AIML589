# KŌRAU VETERINARY CLINIC

*Clinical note | 18 April 2026 | Dr M. Reeves*

Three dogs from the same household were examined for skin irritation during
April. The owner reported a change in coat condition and says the timing
coincided with a change in the household's water supply. The clinic has not
tested the household water and cannot identify a cause from the consultation.
The note recommends individual veterinary follow-up and does not support a
public claim that the water treatment caused the symptoms.

The clinic also notes that skin problems have many possible causes, including
parasites, food and environmental allergens. This note records the presenting
symptoms; it is not a water-quality report.
