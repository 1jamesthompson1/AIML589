Authority: national Health New Zealand service information. It describes routes and communication support; it is not a substitute for clinical triage or ambulance assessment.

Healthline is free over the phone, available 24 hours a day, seven days a week. Call 0800 611 116 for advice from experienced nurses and paramedics about what to do next, including when worried or unsure, when there is no GP or one cannot be reached, or when medicine advice is needed.

A Healthline clinician may recommend care at home, seeing a regular GP, monitoring symptoms and calling back if worse, or attending an Urgent Care Centre or hospital ED. Healthline can help find an after-hours GP, ED, pharmacy or after-hours dental surgery, and can connect a caller with an ambulance when needed.

From 8am to 8pm callers may choose to speak with a Māori clinician. Interpreter services are available in up to 150 languages; tell Healthline the language needed so an interpreter can be arranged. NZ Relay Services support Deaf, hearing-impaired, Deafblind or speech-impaired callers and are available for limited hours.

If immediate advice is not needed, a caller can request a callback through the Healthline website. Callers can also use mobile images or video to help explain the concern.
