\subsection{Assignment of respondents to clusters}

At the start of the survey a handful of questions will be asked to determine which cluster the respondent belongs to. The questions selected are most information questions to help assign a respondent to a cluster.

Some questions are considered contreversial and so were omitted from potential cluster assignment questions. As seen in \figref{clusters/k2_analysis/figures/certainty_by_n_questions}, this has little effect on the confidence of the assignment. In the final 10 questions selected \fixmeinline{Add appendix showing questions selected.} we can assignm cluster with a mean of 0.915 and over 74\% of the WVS respondents with over 90\%.
